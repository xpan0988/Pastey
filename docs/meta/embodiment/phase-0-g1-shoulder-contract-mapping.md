## Task authority

**PHASE 0 CONTRACT:** The human reviewing principal approves this exact simulated task and its declared modeled-risk envelope; META Core is the authoritative grant/acceptance owner. A single task grant is tied to exact Plan/revision/approval/attempt, exact `HostRef`, exact bound simulator/controller/enforcer instance, slot `22` and named joint, a parameterized target region strictly within the modeled joint range, permitted PD-plus-feedforward `LowCmd` family (`q`, `dq`, `tau`, `kp`, `kd` bounds), the whole-packet treatment for other 28 motors, monotonic local validity interval, candidate/state freshness, entry/hold/exit predicates, conflict exclusion, and a recovery ceiling of **observe/reconcile only**. It authorizes one target transition, not arbitrary repeated commands, an arm sequence, or an inverse recovery move.

The grant may narrow but never widen the reviewed task. The enforcer checks the current local grant and each full command candidate before publication; Core records admission and effect-governance decisions. An expired approval, wrong attempt/Host/map, unsupported control family, stale state, missing candidate, or unknown publication ownership denies admission or ends task progression. A command published before revocation may still have residual modeled effect; authority termination is not an effect-reversal claim.

A Core revocation **request** and locally enforced revocation are distinct facts. The enforcer must acknowledge when it fenced task publication and began any separately authorized protective response. If that acknowledgment cannot arrive, Core cannot claim local intervention occurred; the preauthorized local expiry and maximum disconnected exposure still bound task authority. The clock/skew and disconnected-exposure limits remain parameters to qualify before admission. Network silence is neither a protective acknowledgment nor evidence that the simulator stopped. [Constitution real-time invariant](constitution.md#real-time-authority-invariant)

**Representation boundary:** Current `AuthorityContextV1` and `EffectEnvelopeV1` are digital-only in their fields. Physical task scope, time, and protective relation must eventually lower into **the same authoritative META effect-governance spine and decision/evidence history**, with an explicit versioned physical boundary; this is not a fifth `EffectEnvelopeV1` ceiling and not an `AuthorityLeaseStore`. The exact type, durable representation, and local grant projection remain unresolved. [Current context and envelope](../../../src-tauri/src/effect_authority.rs#L77), [reuse D6](decision-record-v0.1.md)

## Protective authority

**PHASE 0 CONTRACT — `DampedCommandV0` candidate:** The reviewing principal must authorize a **separate**, simulation-only standing protective envelope as part of admitting this bound instance. META Core grants and records it through the shared decision spine; the local enforcer alone invokes it. If task authority expires/is revoked, candidates stop, state/candidate freshness fails, or writer ownership becomes uncertain, the enforcer forbids further task packets and—only while its own protective grant is valid—may replace task progression with a bounded damping-like stream. For the affected motor, `kp=0`, desired `dq=0`, feedforward `tau=0`, and a declared positive bounded `kd` make the inspected simulator formula oppose sensed velocity. This is **not** SafeStop, EmergencyStop, SafeMode, certified damping, hardware protection, or a guaranteed stable pose. [Bridge formula](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_mujoco/blob/1eb6642e3f3fdfb7fb13a9794fd6a2dd93ea0e7d/simulate/src/unitree_sdk2_bridge.h#L161-L180)

The protective grant needs its own start triggers, maximum local duration, command precedence, full 29-motor packet policy, `kd` domain, candidate/observation clock, response-start bound, and residual-effect observation. The enforcer must record trigger, local acknowledgment, attempted publication, and fresh `q/dq/tau_est` response; a DDS write alone proves no damping effect. Failure to publish, inability to exclude another writer, no velocity decay under a declared modeled criterion, stale observation, or expiry of protective authority yields **protective outcome unknown/failed and escalation**, never an accepted stop. Protection may outlive task authority only within its own interval and cannot resume the task. Once it expires, no task motion may restart without new authorization; the simulator may keep applying the last packet.

**UNRESOLVED blocker:** The source supports a damping-like *formula* for a motor but does not establish an adequate full-body protective packet when the native controller stops, a precedence mechanism against another DDS writer, or a bounded response/residual-effect guarantee. Those semantics must be decided and shown enforceable in the Phase 0 harness before prototype admission. No physical stopping bound is asserted.

## Local enforcement ownership

**Sole publication owner** means that, for the chosen DDS domain/interface/topic and simulator instance, every task-authorized HG `LowCmd` packet reaches `rt/lowcmd` through one enforcer-owned publisher after local checks. The native controller can supply only candidates to that owner; it cannot independently publish task packets. The enforcer must control and verify this harness property before admission and throughout execution. DDS itself provides no source-backed exclusive writer or priority guarantee. Merely naming a sole owner is insufficient; process configuration, publisher discovery/control, and a fail-closed evidence rule are required. [Reconnaissance enforcement surface](g1-mujoco-platform-reconnaissance.md#enforcement-surface), [pinned simulator subscription](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_mujoco/blob/1eb6642e3f3fdfb7fb13a9794fd6a2dd93ea0e7d/simulate/src/unitree_sdk2_bridge.h#L141-L155)

If exclusive ownership cannot be established at entry, **inadmissible**. If it becomes uncertain mid-attempt or a competing publisher appears, task authority is invalid locally: reject all new task candidates, mark attribution of `LowState` effects uncertain, attempt only separately authorized protection if protective publication can itself be established, and reconcile from fresh observation. A competing publisher is an authority breach and evidence-confounder even if its target equals the reviewed target. This document chooses no concrete controller/enforcer IPC or DDS publisher-discovery technique.

## Completion: Disposition → Observed Effect → Acceptance

1. **Disposition (enforcer/authority fact):** Core and enforcer record whether the attempt was never admitted, admitted, each candidate allowed/denied/unavailable, each packet attempted for publication, task authority ended, and protection was requested/attempted. An allowed effect decision or DDS write says nothing by itself about simulated actuation. The current digital `EffectDecisionV1` provides the decision meaning, not a physical completion status. [Current decision](../../../src-tauri/src/effect_authority.rs#L1247)
2. **Observed Effect (qualified simulation fact):** A verifier evaluates fresh slot-22 `q/dq/tau_est` traces against the reviewed target and `T_settle`; optional declared base/contact witnesses qualify broader effects. It records partial movement, target crossing without settling, stale/contradictory evidence, or unknown effect. `tau_est` is a model sensor value. No `LowState` sample itself proves the intended command caused the position.
3. **Acceptance (META Core verdict):** Core may accept only the exact reviewed simulated shoulder effect when disposition and qualified observed effect agree, the bound attempt remains eligible, no revocation/intervention forbids acceptance, and witness sufficiency is met. It can record “effect verified but intent unaccepted” where posture evidence exists but authority/correlation failed. Later evidence creates a linked revised verdict, not silent historical replacement. [Current digital acceptance pattern](../../../src-tauri/src/managed_execution.rs#L699), [reuse D8](decision-record-v0.1.md)

## Failure and terminal outcomes

This path permits **never admitted; admitted with no confirmed actuation; partially effective; target crossed but not settled; observation stale; effect unknown; protective transition entered; protective outcome failed/unknown; effect verified but intent unaccepted; accepted**. These are semantic dispositions/observations/verdicts for this path, not a new universal enum. A terminal nonacceptance may coexist with continued physical evolution or protective observation. A stale state never becomes inferred success; command cessation never becomes inferred stopping; one accepted shoulder angle never becomes whole-body safety.

## Recovery and reconciliation

After expiry, cancellation, writer conflict, stale observation, process death, or a partial/unknown result, no original task resumes merely because a process restarts or a message returns. Core fences the old attempt; the local enforcer uses only remaining separately granted protection, if available. Reconciliation requires fresh binding and qualified current simulator observation, with explicit distinction among last known command, last reported `LowState`, actual current `q/dq`/posture, publisher ownership, and unknown effects. A new movement or retry requires a new reviewed attempt and authority; no blind inverse command or automatic return to the initial angle is authorized.

**Analogy, not reuse of a physical implementation:** Pastey's Native Agent retains execution truth separately from Return/apply consequence truth, keeps exact identities through restart, and reconciles ambiguous outcomes without rerunning the Agent. The embodiment path reuses no-rerun, cancellation fencing, exact correlation, and explicit reconciliation **semantics**; it cannot treat a retained file tree or digital Return proof as physical state. [Native Agent restart/no-rerun](../../../src-tauri/src/native_agent.rs#L535), [correlated reconciliation](../../../src-tauri/src/native_agent.rs#L2041), [task-authority stop](../../../src-tauri/src/native_agent.rs#L3446)

## Falsification matrix

In each case the enforcer fences **future task packets**; it cannot retract an already applied `ctrl`. “Protection” below means only the separately granted simulator-scoped candidate, subject to sole-writer and full-packet feasibility. `D` = Disposition, `O` = Observed Effect, `A` = Acceptance. All outcomes correlate to the original attempt and preserve its history.

| Case | Authority state / task progression | Protective behavior | Evidence state; D → O → A | Recovery / reconciliation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **F1 expiry during motion** | Task grant expired on Host monotonic clock; no new task packet. | Invoke `DampedCommandV0` only if separate protection remains valid and publishable; stopping publication alone is insufficient. | `D`: task expiry and protective trigger/attempt; `O`: fresh q/dq may show continued/partial motion or unknown; `A`: no success solely from pre-expiry commands, possibly verified effect but unaccepted. | Reobserve current posture/residual motion; no old-attempt resumption; new review/attempt for movement. |
| **F2 controller stops candidates** | Candidate watchdog fails; task progression forbidden even if its grant has time left. | Enforcer must supply the declared full protective packet independently of missing native candidates; if it cannot, protective outcome unknown. | `D`: candidate loss and intervention attempt; `O`: q/dq may remain moving because bridge retains last packet; `A`: no accepted transition without qualified settled evidence. | Fresh observation, controller/binding check, new attempt; never treat silence as stop. |
| **F3 `LowState` stale** | Continuing freshness unknown/false; task packets stop. | Protective stream only under own grant and credible writer ownership; response cannot be verified while state is stale. | `D`: stale-state fence/possible publication; `O`: unknown from that point; `A`: unaccepted or deferred, never inferred success. | Restore qualified state and reconcile actual posture; new authorization for movement. |
| **F4 competing publisher / loss of exclusivity** | Task authority locally invalid; enforcer rejects task candidates. | If protective writer priority/exclusivity is also unprovable, do not claim protection applied. | `D`: breach detected; `O`: command attribution and possibly effect unknown even with `LowState`; `A`: no accepted task effect on conflicted evidence. | Remove conflict, prove sole ownership, obtain fresh independent observation, reconcile; new attempt required. |
| **F5 META/enforcer restart mid-action** | Old local task grant cannot be presumed live; no automatic publication or replay. | Existing protective action/persistence uncertain; newly running enforcer needs valid separate authority before any packet. | `D`: last durable decision plus interruption; `O`: past sample historical, current effect unknown until fresh witness; `A`: no resurrection of prior success. | Fence attempt, rebind instance/writer, observe actual state, reconcile old attempt; new attempt for further motion. |
| **F6 target crossed, `dq` never settles** | Grant may continue only until its declared deadline/conditions; no automatic extension. | At deadline, task packets stop; protective candidate if separately authorized and needed. | `D`: commands admitted or deadline reached; `O`: target crossing/possibly partial effect, no `T_settle` window; `A`: not accepted. | Observe current motion; new reviewed task if further transition desired. |
| **F7 damping packet issued, no modeled decay** | Task remains invalid; protection cannot become task authority. | Record response failure or unknown; no fabricated safe state or unlimited protective stream. | `D`: protective publication attempt; `O`: fresh `dq` violates declared decay criterion, or stale yields unknown; `A`: no accepted protection/effect claim. | Escalate, keep task fenced, inspect current whole-body state; further movement needs new authorization. |
| **F8 model/motor map changes after Review** | Exact bound instance mismatch, so old grant cannot be used; no task packet. | Only protection whose own binding still matches may act; otherwise unknown/unavailable. | `D`: admission denied or mid-attempt fenced; `O`: slot-22 samples cannot be attributed under old map; `A`: old reviewed shoulder intent unaccepted. | Rebind/review against actual model/map; fresh observation and new attempt. |

Future tests may support only a statement as narrow as: **“The bound simulated right-shoulder transition survived the declared modeled authority-loss and observation-failure cases under the pinned simulator, controller, enforcer, parameters, and oracle.”** That requires implemented fault injections, observed traces, and a suitable witness; this document alone supplies no test evidence. ## Open questions

### Must resolve before prototype

1. **Exclusive publication:** What concrete harness control and continuously checked evidence establish the enforcer as the sole `rt/lowcmd` publisher, including after restart and in the presence of an unexpected DDS writer? If this cannot be established, admission is denied.
2. **Protective authority and complete packet:** Who grants the separate standing protective envelope in the actual reviewed workflow, how is it narrowed into the one authority spine, and what complete 29-motor packet can the enforcer emit if the native controller disappears? Define precedence, duration, response-start and residual-observation rules without claiming a safe stop.
3. **Adequate continuing witness:** Which fresh `LowState` and, if necessary, direct MuJoCo facts establish the reviewed starting/support region and continuing whole-body condition? If only shoulder `q/dq` can be evaluated, narrow the claim and admission region accordingly. Set a credible clock/stall rule and missing-evidence response.
4. **Restart and local grant continuity:** What durable/locally checkable authority and publisher-ownership evidence permits protection after enforcer restart, or is all action fenced pending fresh Core authorization? No current process-local `EffectAuthorityStateV1` automatically reconstructs a physical grant.

### Can remain open during simulation Phase 0, but must be fixed before an individual run or claim

- Exact physical authority/Plan wire representation and controller-to-enforcer private IPC, provided the single Core authority owner and no-bypass semantics above remain intact.
- Qualified numerical `R_target`, `R_entry`, `R_hold`, `E_q`, `E_dq`, `T_state`, `T_candidate`, `T_settle`, `T_task`, `T_protect`, protective `K_d`, and response/decay criteria. They must be recorded in the reviewed run before admission, not guessed by an adapter.
- Whether a direct MuJoCo witness is needed for a **joint-only** effect claim versus a broader support/contact claim; witness independence and provenance must match the final claim.
- Future physical-device identity, protective authority, calibration, firmware evidence, and real stopping semantics. Simulation cannot settle these.

## Phase 0 prototype gate

**NOT YET PASS for beginning the first simulator implementation.** The source gives a precise motor mapping, command formula, and reported joint observation, and this document assigns semantic owners. It does **not** yet establish (1) enforceable sole publication ownership, (2) separately authorized and executable full-packet protection when native candidates vanish, (3) a qualified continuing/support witness with clock/staleness rules, or (4) restart authority/ownership behavior. Those are minimum architecture decisions, not values to let an adapter invent.

For each authority-bearing predicate and physical response, the required test remains: **who evaluates; who enforces; what evidence; which host/simulation clock and freshness bound; and what happens if missing or uncertain?** Until the four decisions above have concrete, mutually consistent answers, local admission for this capability is **inadmissible**. Representation details may remain open where semantic ownership and fail-closed behavior are already unambiguous.
